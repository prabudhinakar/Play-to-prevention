## Project

A 13-year-old streamer gets "marked" by the one person who's supposed to protect him: his big sister, who also happens to be his moderator.

---

## Pitch

Mark is 13, a rising Twitch streamer who's dealing with puberty and a popularity he never asked for. His sister Hannah, 15, used to be the family's golden child, and now she watches their parents rave about him. So she starts humiliating him with embarrassing clips and comments, and her mod powers make it spread faster. How far will she go to get her family's attention back, and what happens when breaking her brother is the only way she feels seen?

---

## Setup

**Where:** An ordinary suburban home. In the living room, the parents cheer for Mark. Online, the same people watch, clip, and share.

**Perspective:** The chat tells the story, as a crowd of usernames, emotes, clips, and mod logs. It talks straight to *you*, the viewer, and asks what you did while you watched.

**Tension:** Hannah should protect Mark, but she becomes the biggest source of his humiliation. Every clip she posts pushes his view count up, so her cruelty feeds the success she resents. The more he grows, the angrier she gets, and eventually she can't control what she started.

**Name play:**
- **Mark** is *marked*: tagged, clipped, singled out. He goes from "Mark" to "a mark," a target.
- **Hannah** is a palindrome, the same forwards and backwards, like a mirror. She sees herself in her brother and hates it.

**Puberty, twice:** Mark's changes are public, with his voice, skin and body on camera. Hannah's are private. Her first periods came late, after her friends', and she hides the pain and the mood swings because nobody asks. Midway through, her period starts at a bad moment and someone records it. Mark, the brother she's been humiliating, quietly helps her. Now she knows what being exposed feels like, and the story ends on her choice: delete the clips, or let the chat decide.

## Personas

### Mark (13), the streamer
- **Wants:** To be good at the game, to be liked, and to feel okay in his changing body.
- **Struggles with:** Body image, gaming pressure, and sensory overload from the constant noise, notifications and chat. He can't turn the audience off, even when he's shaking.
- **Conflict:** With himself, because the more people watch him, the less he recognises himself. With Hannah, whose betrayal he can't name because she's family.

### Hannah (15), the older sister and moderator
- **Wants:** To be noticed and chosen again, like before Mark.
- **Going through:** Her first periods, with pain, mood swings, and the feeling of being behind everyone.
- **Archetype twist:** The golden child and protector turns into the saboteur who thinks she's the victim.
- **Conflict:** She wants her brother's pain to stop and also wants it to continue, because it's the only thing that gets her looked at. She steers the conversations and uses her mod powers, then can't undo what the chat turns into.

### The parents
- **Want:** A family success story.
- **Conflict:** They praise Mark's numbers instead of seeing him, and they've stopped looking at Hannah at all.
