# Find the bully
- A choice based interactive drama game where you play like a teenager and go around places and bully / get bullied based on your choices to create awareness.

![alt text](<WhatsApp Image 2026-10-19 at 2.27.50 PM.jpeg>)


## The Idea
- The game is played by making choices that reflect how we would react in a given situation and discovering the consequences of those choices. We learn to understand each situation from another person’s perspective. Depending on our decisions, we may take on the role of the bully, the victim, or the bystander. Each choice leads to different outcomes, influencing the story and the relationships between characters.
- By experiencing different perspectives, players develop empathy and become more aware of how their actions can affect others.

## Lists
- It is a story based game, where all the scenes are based on your actions, and your actions decide the climax of the story.
- we get to navigate through multiple areas using mini map and each area has a drama waiting for us.
- Choice based Branching Dialogues and storyline.
- Discover how your actions affect other characters, their emotions, relationships.
- After the game completes and gives the summary of what happened and we get to read the full picture.
 
## Possible Locations and Scenarios
- Classroom: A student is mocked after giving a wrong answer.
- School Corridor: A group deliberately excludes someone from a conversation.
- Cafeteria: Friends laugh at a humiliating photo of another student.
- School Playground: A student is pressured to join in teasing someone.
- Group Chat: An embarrassing screenshot is shared without permission.
- Online Gaming Lobby: A player is insulted and targeted by other players.
- Bus Stop: A bystander witnesses bullying but fears becoming the next target.

# The main goal
- Building empathy through experience. Rather than telling players what is right or wrong, Find the Bully allows them to experience the consequences of their choices, understand different perspectives, and recognise how small actions can contribute to or prevent bullying.
