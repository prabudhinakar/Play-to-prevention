Welcome. Today: what cyberbullying is, why it hurts, how play helps us prevent it, and what to do.

What is cyberbullying?

Repeated, hurtful behaviour toward someone using phones, games, social media or messages.

Harassment

Insults, threats and nasty messages

Exclusion

Leaving someone out of chats and groups on purpose

Impersonation

Pretending to be someone else to embarrass them

Spreading content

Sharing rumours, private photos or screenshots

Why is it different?

  • Always on: it follows you home, day and night
  • Big audience: one post can reach hundreds of people
  • Permanent: screenshots and shares are hard to take back
  • Hidden: bullies can be anonymous, and adults often don't see it

Why play?

Games let us practise real choices where mistakes are safe.

Empathy

Play the target and see how a message feels

Choices

Every decision has a visible consequence

Courage

Rehearse speaking up before it really happens

People remember what they experience more than what they are told.

If it happens to you

  1. Pause. Don't reply or hit back
  2. Save screenshots as evidence
  3. Block and report on the platform
  4. Tell a trusted adult or friend

If you see it happen

  • Don't like, share or laugh along
  • Reach out privately to the person targeted
  • Report it, and tell an adult you trust
  • Be the upstander, not the bystander

You are not alone. Speaking up helps.
